What the price includes

Mzigua Safari Tours packages include all Kilimanjaro National Park fees (about 35% of the total cost), plus:

  • Meals for customers
  • Meals for the crew: guides, porters and cooks
  • Sleeping tents for customers
  • Mess tents for eating
  • Medical tent for first aid
  • Tents for the crew
  • Kitchen tents for cooking
  • Lunch box on the first day and the last day
  • Mattresses for sleeping
  • Breakfast, lunch, dinner and fruit every day on the mountain
  • Fresh food supplies brought from town, usually on day 4 and day 5
  • Free transfers from Kilimanjaro International Airport until you fly home

Tips for the crew

Our porters, cooks and guides are day workers with no steady employment. We pay their wages on every Kilimanjaro trip, but because of rising taxes in Tanzania the pay is low and does not support their families or their future. Your tips go directly to the team and help them build a better life. Please give tips per day, in these categories:

CrewOur daily wageSuggested tip
Porters10,000 TZS per day$7 per day
Cooks / chef15,000 TZS per day$10 per day
Guides20,000 TZS per day$15 per day

You are welcome to add more for very good service. A happy, motivated team helps you reach Uhuru Peak (5,895 m), earn your summit certificate and stand on the highest mountain in Africa.

Luggage note. Your main bag must not weigh more than 15 kg. The chairs and sleeping mattress add about 5 kg more, and your porters carry all of it, together with your food and sleeping tents. On summit day a porter, the chief guide and an assistant guide stay with you to support you to the top.
